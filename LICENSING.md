# Licensing FAQ

Plain-language answers about how Fleet is licensed. **This is not legal advice.** If this page and [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) disagree, `LICENSE.md` governs.

## Is Fleet open source?

No. It is **source-available**. You can read the code, but the license is not an open source license. Every OSI-approved license must allow redistribution and derived works, and this one doesn't.

The license is the [PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).

## Can my company use it?

Yes, for free. Any organization may run Fleet for its own internal business operations. That includes the owner's employer.

## Can we change it?

Yes. You may make changes for your own internal use, for example patching it to fit your CI.

## What isn't allowed?

- Redistributing the software, modified or not.
- Publishing public forks of modified versions.
- Reselling it, rebranding it, or offering it to third parties, including as a hosted service.

## Can I fork it on GitHub?

Yes, **to open a pull request**. That's expected and fine. GitHub's Terms of Service already let users fork public repositories on GitHub. A fork gives you no rights outside GitHub and no right to distribute the software.

## How do I contribute?

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Every contributor signs a Contributor License Agreement first:

- [Individual CLA](CLA.md), signed by a pull request comment.
- [Corporate CLA](CLA-CORPORATE.md) for work done on company time, because the employer owns that work.

The CLA gives the owner a broad license to your contribution, including the right to sublicense and relicense. It is **not** a copyright assignment: you keep copyright in your work.

## Can I donate?

Yes, thank you. Use the **Sponsor** button on the repository (GitHub Sponsors). **Donations buy no license rights.** They don't grant redistribution rights, support obligations or priority.

## What about the dependencies?

Fleet links only permissively licensed crates (checked by `cargo deny`; see [`deny.toml`](deny.toml)). Release artifacts ship their notices in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.html`, generated by `cargo about`.

The reference deployment runs [Garage](https://garagehq.deuxfleurs.fr/) (AGPLv3) as a **separate service** reached over the S3 API. Fleet does not link it. The AGPL obligations apply to Garage only. SeaweedFS (Apache-2.0) is a documented alternative.
