# Licensing FAQ

Plain-language answers about how Fleet is licensed. **This is not legal advice.** If this page and [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/moyaver/fleet/blob/main/LICENSE) disagree, `LICENSE` governs.

## Is Fleet open source?

Yes. Fleet is licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/moyaver/fleet/blob/main/LICENSE), `AGPL-3.0-only`, an OSI-approved open source license.

## Can my company use it?

Yes, for free, for any purpose, including running it internally for your own CI. That includes the owner's employer.

## Can we change it?

Yes. Modify it however you like.

## What does the AGPL ask in return?

If you **distribute** Fleet (modified or not), or let people **use a modified Fleet over a network**, you must offer them the complete corresponding source code of your version under the AGPL. That's what stops anyone taking Fleet closed: every copy and every hosted variant stays open.

Running an unmodified Fleet, or a modified one only you use, asks nothing of you.

## Can I fork it?

Yes, it's open source. Forks are welcome under the AGPL. A fork must not use the name "Fleet" in a way that suggests it is the official project, or that the owner endorses it. Call your fork something else, and say it's based on Fleet.

## I want to use Fleet in a proprietary product

The AGPL doesn't allow that without releasing your source. A **commercial license** with different terms is available: email <mhammadjaber00@outlook.com>.

## How do I contribute?

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/moyaver/fleet/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). Every contributor signs a Contributor License Agreement first:

- [Individual CLA](https://github.com/moyaver/fleet/blob/main/CLA.md), signed by a pull request comment.
- [Corporate CLA](https://github.com/moyaver/fleet/blob/main/CLA-CORPORATE.md) for work done on company time, because the employer owns that work.

The CLA gives the owner a broad license to your contribution, including the right to sublicense and relicense. That's what makes the commercial license possible. It is **not** a copyright assignment: you keep copyright in your work, and your contribution is always available under the AGPL too.

## Can I donate?

Yes, thank you. Use the **Sponsor** button on the repository (GitHub Sponsors). Donations don't buy a commercial license, support obligations or priority.

## What about the dependencies?

Fleet links only crates whose licenses are on the allow list in [`deny.toml`](https://github.com/moyaver/fleet/blob/main/deny.toml), checked by `cargo deny`. The list holds permissive licenses, plus MPL-2.0 for unmodified use. That keeps the commercial license possible. Release artifacts ship their notices in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.html`, generated by `cargo about`.

The reference deployment runs [Garage](https://garagehq.deuxfleurs.fr/) (AGPLv3) as a **separate service** reached over the S3 API. Fleet does not link it.
